QuestLHSync for the PICO 4 Pro - SteamVR driver (experimental community port)

1. Unpack this folder somewhere it can stay, e.g. C:\QuestLHSync-pico.
   SteamVR loads the driver from here: don't move or delete it afterwards.
2. Close SteamVR. In PowerShell, in this folder:
     powershell -executionpolicy bypass -file register_driver.ps1
   (-remove after it unregisters the driver.)
3. Install the headset module (QuestLHSync-pico-module-*.zip) in Magisk and
   reboot the headset.
4. Turn off SpaceCalibrator, OpenVR-SpaceSync's calibration and anything else
   that moves lighthouse devices. Start SteamVR, wear your trackers or hold
   your controllers, and look at each base station for a while.

Full guide, troubleshooting and the source:
https://github.com/UndefinedSignal/QuestLHSync-pico4pro/blob/pico-4-pro/pico/README.md
